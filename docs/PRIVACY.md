# Privacy Policy (summary)

**Full version:** served at `/privacy` on the deployed site · source: `apps/web/src/app/privacy/page.tsx`  
**Last updated:** 1 June 2026 · Version 2.0

DomainTLSProbe processes probe targets you submit, minimal technical logs (IP, rate limits), and optional alert subscription emails. We do not sell personal data. Probe history is in-memory and cleared on restart.

For GDPR/UK GDPR we rely on legitimate interests and contract; for alerts, consent. You may have access/deletion rights depending on your jurisdiction — contact via GitHub issues on this repository.
